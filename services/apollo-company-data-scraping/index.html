Apollo Company Data Scraping FAQ

Common questions about this service.

What company details are included?

Name, website, industry, employee range, revenue range when available, and headquarters location.

Can you also get the people at these companies?

Yes. Combine this with Apollo Data Scraping to get contacts at each company.

Can I filter by technology or funding?

Yes, when the filter is available in Apollo. Tell us what you need and we confirm before starting.

How do I receive the list?

As a Google Sheet shared with you, or a CSV file.
