Apollo Email Enrichment FAQ

Common questions about this service.

What should my list contain?

A full name and a company website or domain for each person.

Do you verify emails I already have?

Yes. Send them and we return a status for each one.

What does a risky status mean?

The address may exist but could bounce or belong to a catch-all domain. Send to risky emails in small batches.

Do you keep my original columns?

Yes. We add new columns and leave your data as it was.
