LinkedIn Sales Navigator Scraping FAQ

Common questions about this service.

What do you need from me?

A Sales Navigator search link, or a description of your audience so we can help you build the search.

Can you add emails to this list?

Yes. Add Apollo Email Enrichment to get business emails for these leads.

How many leads can one search return?

Sales Navigator limits how many results a single search shows. For large audiences we split the work into several searches.

How is the data delivered?

As a Google Sheet shared with you, or a CSV file.
