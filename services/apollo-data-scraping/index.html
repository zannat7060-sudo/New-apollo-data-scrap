Apollo Data Scraping

About this service

Share your target job titles, industries, locations and company size, or send an Apollo search link. We run the search, collect every matching contact and organize the results into consistent columns.

Each list is checked for duplicates and formatted so you can import it straight into your cold email tool or CRM.

What is included

  • Name, job title, company and location
  • Business email with a status for each contact
  • LinkedIn profile link when available
  • Duplicate rows removed
  • Google Sheet or CSV delivery

Apollo Data Scraping FAQ

Common questions about this service.

What do I need to send?

Your target job titles, industries, countries and company size, or an Apollo search link. We can also help you build the filters.

How many leads can I order?

Small test lists and large lists are both possible. Tell us the quantity and we confirm price and timing before starting.

Are the emails verified?

We check emails and mark each one as valid, risky or invalid, so you can choose what to send.

What format do I get?

A Google Sheet shared with you, or a CSV file if you prefer.
