What clients say

Questions we hear most

Cannot find your answer? Send us a message.

What do you need from me to start?

Your target audience (job titles, industries, countries, company size), an Apollo or Sales Navigator search link if you have one, the quantity you need and the columns you want. If you are unsure, send what you have and we help with the rest.

How will I receive the data?

As a Google Sheet shared with you, or as a CSV file. Columns are consistent and ready to import into your outreach tool.

How long does delivery take?

Small lists often take one to three business days. Larger lists depend on size and filters. We confirm the timing before you pay.

How do you check accuracy?

Emails are checked and marked valid, risky or invalid. Phone numbers get a status. Duplicate rows are removed before delivery.

Can I see a sample first?

Yes. Look at the sample sheet on this page, or ask for a small sample that matches your audience.

How do I pay?

We share payment details after you approve the scope and sample. Ask us about the options available in your country.
